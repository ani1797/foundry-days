# NHL-POL-101 Mortgage Eligibility Policy
Northwind Home Lending (fictional). Version 3.2, effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Credit Policy.

## 1. Who can apply
- Applicants must be 18 or older, legal residents of Canada, and the age of majority in their province.
- Maximum of 4 borrowers per application.
- Non-resident applicants are referred to a human underwriter (see NHL-POL-106).

## 2. Property types
- Eligible: owner-occupied detached, semi-detached, townhouse, condominium (up to 4 units).
- Rental properties require a minimum 20% down payment.
- Properties used for commercial purposes over 49% of floor area are not eligible.

## 3. Down payment
| Purchase price | Minimum down payment |
|---|---|
| Up to $500,000 | 5% |
| $500,000.01 to $999,999 | 5% of the first $500,000 plus 10% of the remainder |
| $1,000,000 and above | 20% |

Down payments below 20% require mortgage default insurance.

## 4. Credit score bands
| Band | Score | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| excellent | 760+ | standard pricing |
| good | 700-759 | standard pricing |
| fair | 640-699 | pricing premium of 0.25 percentage points; refer if LTV above 80% |
| poor | below 640 | refer to underwriter; no automated pre-qualification |

## 5. Employment
Minimum 2 years in the same field. Self-employed applicants need 2 years of Notice of Assessment history and are always referred for manual review.

## 6. Pre-qualification is not approval
A pre-qualification result is an indication only. It is not a commitment to lend. Final approval needs verified documents and underwriter sign-off.
