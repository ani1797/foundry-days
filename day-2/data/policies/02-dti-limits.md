# NHL-POL-102 Debt Service Ratio (DTI) Limits
Version 2.4, effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Credit Policy.

## 1. Definitions
- **GDS** (gross debt service) = (mortgage principal and interest + property tax + heating + 50% of condo fees) / gross monthly income.
- **TDS** (total debt service) = (GDS housing costs + all other monthly debt payments) / gross monthly income.

## 2. Limits
| Credit band | Max GDS | Max TDS |
|---|---|---|
| excellent / good | 39% | 44% |
| fair | 35% | 42% |
| poor | not eligible for automated pre-qualification | |

## 3. Qualifying rate
Ratios are calculated at the greater of the contract rate plus 2.00 percentage points, or 5.25%.

## 4. Decision rules for the assistant
- Both ratios at or below limits and LTV at or below 80%: result **likely**.
- Either ratio within 3 points above the limit, or LTV between 80% and 95%: result **refer** to an underwriter.
- Otherwise: result **unlikely**; explain which measure failed.
- The assistant must never describe any result as an approval.

## 5. Rental income
Only 50% of verified rental income counts towards income. Unverified rental income counts for nothing.
