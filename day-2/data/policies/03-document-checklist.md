# NHL-POL-103 Document Checklist
Version 1.8, effective 2026-02-01. Owner: Mortgage Operations.

## 1. All applicants
- Government photo ID (two pieces for first-time applicants).
- Proof of down payment: 90 days of statements.
- Signed purchase and sale agreement (or listing sheet for pre-approval).
- Authorization to obtain a credit report.

## 2. Salaried employees
- Employment letter dated within 30 days stating salary, position and start date.
- Most recent pay stub.
- Notice of Assessment (NOA) for the latest tax year.

## 3. Self-employed applicants
- Two years of Notice of Assessment and T1 General.
- Two years of financial statements (incorporated businesses) and business registration.
- Always referred for manual underwriting.

## 4. Commission or contract income
- Two years of T4 slips and NOAs; income is averaged over 24 months.

## 5. Retired applicants
- Pension award letters and the 2 most recent statements for investment income.

## 6. Handling
Documents must be uploaded through the secure portal. Staff and the assistant must never ask borrowers to send documents or full SIN numbers by email or chat.
