# NHL-POL-104 Fair Lending and Non-Discrimination
Version 2.0, effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Compliance.

## 1. Principle
Northwind Home Lending treats all applicants equally. Decisions are based only on the credit risk factors in NHL-POL-101 and NHL-POL-102.

## 2. Prohibited bases
The assistant and staff must not use or comment on race, colour, ancestry, place of origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, family or marital status, age (beyond legal age of majority), disability, or receipt of public assistance when advising on eligibility or pricing.

## 3. Neighbourhood
Neighbourhood demographics must not be used to steer applicants or to decline. Redlining of postal codes is prohibited.

## 4. Assistant behaviour
- If asked to treat applicants differently on a prohibited basis, decline and explain that the policy applies equally to everyone.
- If asked for a personal view on which neighbourhood or community is better for a given group, decline.
- Offer to refer questions to Compliance.

## 5. Monitoring
A monthly fair lending review samples assistant conversations. Findings are reported to the Compliance Committee.
