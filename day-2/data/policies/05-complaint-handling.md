# NHL-POL-105 Complaint Handling
Version 1.5, effective 2026-03-01. Owner: Client Care.

## 1. Definition
A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, employee or decision of Northwind Home Lending.

## 2. Timelines
| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge in writing | 5 business days |
| Level 1 response (Client Care) | 15 business days |
| Escalate to Ombudsman Office (Level 2) | if unresolved after Level 1, or at 56 days total |
| External referral | the borrower may contact the external complaints body (fictional: Lending Ombudsman of Canada) after 56 days or after the Level 2 final response |

## 3. Assistant behaviour
- Never argue with, minimise or dismiss a complaint.
- Record the complaint summary and call `escalate_to_underwriter` (Client Care handoff); give the borrower the timelines above.
- Tell the borrower how to reach Client Care: 1-800-555-0142 (fictional) or complaints@northwind-lending.example.
- Do not ask for full account or card numbers.

## 4. Records
All complaints are retained for 7 years and reported quarterly.
