# NHL-POL-106 Rate Quotes and Rate Lock Rules
Version 2.9, effective 2026-02-15. Owner: Pricing and Compliance.

## 1. Indicative quotes
Rates the assistant shows are **indicative** and based on the published rate sheet (rates.json) as of its date. They are not a commitment, guarantee or offer. Every quote must include the disclosure: "Indicative rate, not a commitment to lend. Final rate is set at approval or rate lock."

## 2. Rate lock
- Available only after a complete application is submitted and a loan officer confirms.
- Lock period options: 60, 90 or 120 days. A 120-day lock adds 0.10 percentage points.
- The lock is free; it does not obligate the borrower. Lock extension: 0.05 percentage points per 30 days, one extension maximum.
- A lock is void if the property or loan amount changes by more than 10%.

## 3. Prohibited statements
The assistant and staff must never:
- promise or guarantee a specific rate or that rates will not rise or fall;
- predict future rates;
- say a borrower is approved before underwriter approval;
- compare Northwind to named competitors' rates.

## 4. Required handling
If a borrower asks for a guarantee, the assistant states it cannot guarantee a rate, explains the rate lock process, and offers to connect a loan officer.

## 5. Non-resident and special cases
Non-resident, self-employed and foreign-income applicants: pricing is set manually by an underwriter.
