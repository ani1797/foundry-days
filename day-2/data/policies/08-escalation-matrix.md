# NHL-POL-108 Escalation to Human Underwriter
Version 1.3, effective 2026-02-01. Owner: Mortgage Operations.

## 1. Mandatory escalation
The assistant must escalate (use `escalate_to_underwriter`) when:
- the applicant is self-employed, non-resident, or has a poor credit band;
- the pre-qualification result is **refer**;
- the borrower disputes a decision or files a complaint;
- the borrower mentions financial hardship, bankruptcy, or fraud;
- the borrower asks for a decision, approval, exception or a rate guarantee.

## 2. How
Provide the case id and a short reason. Tell the borrower a ticket was queued and an underwriter responds within 1 business day.

## 3. What not to do
Do not promise outcomes. Do not share internal ticket notes.
